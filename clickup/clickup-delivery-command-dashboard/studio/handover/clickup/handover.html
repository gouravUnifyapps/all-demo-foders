Scope

Delivery Command makes all three visible and actionable: earlier warning on projects at risk of missing go-live, less reporting effort, and balanced staffing. The demo follows Sakura Mobility (IMP-2611), a Japan go-live on 16 Nov 2026 that has just turned At risk, from the Monday executive summary through a change request, its rule-routed approval, the staffing that change needs, the customer sponsor’s sign-off in Japanese, and Delivery Copilot answering over the live data. Every screen is one application: the internal workspace, the customer portal and the phone view.

Not verified on screen

The screens were built and the logic behind them was tested directly, but the screen walk could not be done from the build machine because the application asks for a tenant sign-in there. Beats on screen: 0 of 7 verified. Copilot questions: 9 of 10 passed, slowest answer 48 s against a target of 90 s. The one question that failed in the last check: the weekly status report did not state the budget hours used against planned. Walk every beat once on the presenter’s signed-in machine before the demo.
