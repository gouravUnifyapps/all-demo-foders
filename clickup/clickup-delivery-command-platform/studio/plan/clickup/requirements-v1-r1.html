ClickUp Delivery command platform: requirements

Client Clickup · version 1 · round 1 · harvested 23 Oct 2026 · POC lead and manager Gourav Mahunta

1. Summary

Use caseClickUp Delivery command platform: one application where ClickUp's enterprise implementation team plans, staffs and reports on every customer implementation, customers follow and sign off their rollout in a portal, and an AI assistant writes the routine status work.
Asks added114 in all: 64 P0, 49 P1, 1 out of scope (another use case)
Why P0The BRD says every requirement in §5 is a Must for the first release unless marked Should, §9 lists situations the screens must handle and §12 says the application must not suggest the excluded items. Brand inputs, navigation, data volumes, status words, accessibility, integrations, success measures and stakeholder notes are P1.
Not asksThe personas (§4) and the six invented sample customers (§8: Halden Freight, Corvia Health, Brightmoor University, Kestrel Energy, Sakura Mobility, Ostrander Retail) are context for seed data. The four outcomes in §1 restate the §13 success measures (A103 to A108).

3. Open questions

For the POC lead. Each lists its options; the recommended one is marked.

No.QuestionOptions
1Customer health in the portal: the VP of customer success says "Show customers their health score" (A111); implementation leads say "Customers should never see internal health" (A112).
  1. Portal shows no internal health; customers see stage, milestones and open sign-offs only (C1) (recommended)
  2. Portal shows a customer-facing health score, separate from the internal one
  3. Portal shows the internal health as-is
2Phone editing: consultants ask "Let us edit the timeline straight from the phone" (A110), while M1 lists only today's tasks, status and time on the phone and W3's timeline is a desktop screen.
  1. Phone stays at M1/M2 (today, task status, time, alerts); timeline edits stay on desktop (recommended)
  2. Add a simple date change for one task on the phone, with the go-live preview from W3
  3. Full timeline editing on the phone
3One screen without scrolling: the delivery director wants "every project on one screen without scrolling" (A109), while the BRD expects 180 to 300 projects with paging (A61, BRD P1).
  1. Portfolio opens on health counts, risk grid and go-live timeline that fit one screen; the paged project list sits below (recommended)
  2. A compact one-row-per-project list sized to fit the seeded projects with no paging
  3. Keep the paged list only
4Region colours: the resource manager wants "Colour-code consultants by region everywhere" (A113), while §10 says "Meaning never depends on colour alone" (A90).
  1. Region shown as a labelled chip with a colour on resourcing and portfolio screens, never colour alone (recommended)
  2. Colour-code by region on every screen as asked, each with a text label
  3. No region colours
5Sales' "Add a pricing calculator for upsells" (A114) reads as another use case and sits near §12's exclusion of billing. Is it left out of this demo?
  1. Leave it out of scope; it stays listed under other use cases (recommended)
  2. Promote it (reply promote A114) as a simple calculator screen
6Integrations (Salesforce, Slack, Google Drive, Jira; A53, A92 to A95): the profile allows only connections already on the tenant. How are they shown?
  1. Seeded integration-status rows (last sync, one Sync failed) and a seeded deal-won event; no live connection (recommended)
  2. Use live connections for whichever of the four already exist on orbit.uat, seeded for the rest
  3. Ask the client for sandbox credentials
7Logo: the BRD says "Fetch the official logo" (A3) while the profile's standing answer for no supplied logo is a text wordmark placeholder.
  1. Sample the official logo from clickup.com, as the BRD asks, and list it in the G2 pack (recommended)
  2. Text wordmark placeholder per the profile
8Phone view (M1, M2, bottom tabs): one responsive code app or a separate mobile app?
  1. A phone-width route inside the same code app, shown in a phone frame (recommended)
  2. A separate mobile app
9Portal languages (A44, A87): how far do German and Japanese go in the demo?
  1. Full language switch on every portal screen with translated seeded text and local date/number formats (recommended)
  2. English portal with a working switch on the home screen only
10Data volume: the profile seeds 15 to 20 fictional rows; the BRD describes 180 to 300 projects, about 72 consultants, about 400 risks and a 50,000-row upload (§7).
  1. 15 to 20 projects with the six BRD sample customers, about 12 consultants, enough risks to fill the grid, and a generated 50,000-row CSV with 214 bad rows for C4 (recommended)
  2. Seed at BRD volumes (about 200 projects)
  3. Profile minimum only
11Finance partner (S3) is not among the six personas. Who approves 40-hour-plus changes in the demo?
  1. A seeded finance partner user the presenter can switch to (recommended)
  2. The presenter approves both steps as the delivery director
12Weekly executive email (E4, Monday 08:00 per region): sent for real or shown?
  1. Scheduled automation built; the demo triggers one run to the presenter's inbox (recommended)
  2. Email preview page only, no send
