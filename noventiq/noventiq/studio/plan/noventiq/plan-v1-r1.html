G2 plan · Noventiq · v1 · round 1 · POC-noventiq-noventiq-20261026

One customer key, every number traceable to source

Plan for the 10 November demo · POC lead Gourav Mahunta · manager Mrinal Dixit · written 2 November 2026 · G1 approved by Mrinal Dixit on 2 November

A common data layer over Noventiq's three CRMs, three ERPs, payroll and two time tools, built on synthetic copies of those systems. Noventiq's group finance controller sees one account across the CRMs, pipeline in one set of stages, and gross profit for four quarters with every figure cited to its source rows; a quarter close is refused until the three difficult cases are fixed by their owners; account leads turn cited cross-sell and up-sell suggestions into action plans they can accept, edit, accelerate and roll back; and an assistant answers over the same data with citations, including a change just synced from a source system.

Round-2 open questions got no numbered answers, so this plan follows their recommended options (listed in §4) and carries forward only what still needs a person (§5).

3. Data

Source: synthetic and fictional, shaped like each source system, generated by a seeded script so totals add up and the story numbers are fixed. Masking: none needed: every company, person and id is invented; no emails or phones; payroll held at team level only. Amounts in USD; INR converted at 84.00 per USD, fixed for the POC.

4. Intent and coverage

Proposed intent: both, the outcome first and then how it works, in two hood beats after the lean-forward beat. Half of the manager's G1 asks are about the mechanism (linking, agent context, citations, sync, trace-back: A55 to A59) and the deck asks for the six practical questions answered (A52); the outcome alone would leave them unshown, and a platform-only demo would bury the finance story.
