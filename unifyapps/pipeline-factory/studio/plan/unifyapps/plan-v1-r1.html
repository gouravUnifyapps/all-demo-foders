§4

Intent and coverage

Proposed intent: both. The audience is the factory’s own leadership: they need the outcome first (a patch moving from signal to booked meeting, and the CRO tower explaining a red cell), and, because they will commission the build, one look under the hood at the register, an automation and the agent. Use-case would drop beat H1; platform would add 2 to 4 hood beats.

All 96 open asks are covered: 67 by a beat, 18 by a control on a beat screen, 11 by an open question. Seven asks were marked out of scope at G1 (other use cases: the Atomic Patch View, the Content Factory, Mission Control, the Demo Factory, Value Expansion).

Open questions

Answer by number in the G2 thread. Questions 1 to 6 were asked at G1 and are still open; 7 to 12 are new.

1 Who presents this demo, and to whom (audience, roles, slot)? from G1

  • Recommended: the CEO and CRO staff, at the next CEO / CRO staff review (the blueprint is written for the CEO's direction and the Atomic Patch View's CRO staff review; the architecture diagram is due 'the next CEO review')
  • Profile default: a senior operations leader, 20 minutes, in person, on a projector, presented by the person who asked

2 When is the demo, or the next date with the audience? from G1

  • Recommended: before the blueprint's Week 2 architecture review (task A3, 'the next CEO review'); please give the date
  • Profile default: none set; the plan assumes no fixed date

3 The two documents were posted with no message saying what to build from them. Are all 38 agents, 12 workflows and the human gates asks for this demo, or is the demo a slice? from G1

  • Recommended: keep every component as an ask; G2 picks the beats (for example one pilot patch walked from Gate 1 to Gate 2, the patch and CRO control towers, the to-do list) and lists what is shown as narrated
  • Alternative: name the sub-workflows the demo must show, and defer the rest

4 The blueprint lists 'Decisions to confirm' (pilot patch, factory share, whether a factory-held meeting counts as a New Buyer Meeting, escalation windows, BDR pay, etc.). Should the demo run on the blueprint's stated defaults? from G1

  • Recommended: yes, the blueprint's defaults (factory share 50%, five US patches, alarms rise after 2 and 5 business days, a factory meeting is an intro meeting unless the economic buyer attends)
  • Alternative: the lead gives the values to use

5 The documents name real employees (RevOps, the registry owner, the blueprint and spec authors). Should demo screens and seed data use roles or fictional names? from G1

  • Recommended: roles and fictional names only; no real employee names in seed data
  • Alternative: real first names for the internal audience

6 The blueprint cites sources that were not shared: the October 27 strategic team sync transcript, the Atomic Patch View V2, the BDR Questionnaire, the AI Native Transformation Manifesto and the earlier Lead Engine blueprints. Will any be shared before G2? from G1

  • Recommended: share the Atomic Patch View V2 and the October 27 sync transcript in the channel (they set the control tower and vocabulary the demo should match); the next harvest reads them

7 G2 asks the owner to set the intent. new at G2

  • Recommended: both (the outcome first, then one hood beat: the hand-off register, the Decide Gate 1 automation and the Factory Analyst in the builder, after the CRO tower)

8 Nine asks need a system the tenant has no connection for: Apollo (A47, A53), Sprouts (A34, A52), Gong (A48), inbox placement and blacklist tools (A50, A51), LinkedIn Ads (A72) and a writable HubSpot (A76; the tenant holds only 'UnifyApps Prod (Read Only)', a production system). new at G2
